# Design system: style changes

Every change to how the app looks that the design-system work makes, one entry each, so that any of them can be found, changed or reverted later. The decisions behind them are in [design-system.md](./design-system.md) (D1 to D17) and the order of the work in [design-system-plan.md](./design-system-plan.md).

Each entry says what changed on screen, who decided it, where it lives, the commit, and how to undo it:

- **Owner**: decided by the owner, in a numbered decision or an answer during the work (dated).
- **Plan**: a default the plan took where the decisions were silent, open to the owner to overturn.
- **Agent**: a choice made while implementing, where neither the decisions nor the plan said anything.

Paths are relative to `packages/asm-editor/src/`. An entry is added in the same commit as the change it records. Stage 2 (`e6467dba`) changed nothing on screen (78 screenshot pairs identical) and has no entries.
