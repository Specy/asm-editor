Written on 2026-11-07 after the design interview, and corrected the same day after an independent review against the code. The decisions are in [design-system.md](./design-system.md), cited here as D1 to D17; decision 1 is [ADR 0078](../adr/0078-every-page-wears-the-workbench-s-look-but-the-home-page.md); the vocabulary is in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md) (**Card**, **Tray**, **Popover**); the counts behind it are in the [audit](../research/design-system-audit.md). ## Decisions taken while planning

Taken by default, for the owner to overturn:

1. **Two homes for tokens.** Tokens that do not read a theme colour go in a new `styles/tokens.css` on `:root`, imported by `global.css`: the fonts, the type scale, the radii, the shadows, the durations and easing, `--scrim`, and the two drawn from `currentColor`, `--hover-bg` and `--text-muted` (an unregistered custom property keeps `currentColor` until it is used; in `color` it then reads the parent's colour, in `background-color` the element's own). Tokens that read a theme colour (`--line`, `--line-strong`, `--selected-bg`) are appended to `themeCssVariables()`'s output, so every element that carries a theme carries them too. The audit's `--accent-soft` is left out until something needs it. SCSS helpers (`nested-radius()`, `focus-ring()`) go in `styles/*.scss`, used with `@use` as `tray.scss` is.
2. **Fira Code's 500 default through `@font-face`.** Two rules on `FiraCode-VF.woff2` for the one family, `font-weight: 500` and `font-weight: 501 700`: a request for 300 or 400 draws at 500 and 600 to 900 at 501-700; checked in Chrome on 2026-11-07 (400's ink equals the variable font's 500, 700's its 700), and nothing in the app asks Fira Code for less than 400. `editorFont.ts` still gets `fontWeight: '500'` (D15) for Monaco's own measuring. The variable font at 500 lays about 2% more ink than the static Medium file, so all code text changes very slightly, in stage 1 (D15 says so).
3. **The theme renames are mechanical, in stage 2.** `THEME_PROPS`' `red` and `green` become `danger` and `success`, `TEXT_COLORS`' muted pair a prose pair, `themeCssVariables()` writes `--*-text-prose` in place of `--*-text-muted`, and the `.text-muted` class becomes `.text-prose`; every use moves in the same commit, with no alias left. A stored theme's `red` and `green` entries are renamed where saved themes are read (`themeStore.svelte.ts`, about :236) and, being read-only, take the shared value. `warning` joins `THEME_PROPS` (read-only) and `SHARED_COLORS` in stage 1, as an undefined-variable fix. The `--RGB-*` channels are written until their last `rgba(var(--RGB-…))` use is gone, wherever it is (two are in `InstructionsMenu.svelte:51` and `LecturesMenu.svelte:51`), then stop.
4. **Popover numbers in one place.** `PopoverOutline` (today's `CardOutline`) draws its corners and shadow in SVG from numbers, so `--r-overlay` and `--shadow-3` cannot reach it as CSS. One module, `styles/elevation.ts`, exports the Popover's radius in rem and its shadow `{ y: 8, blur: 24, opacity: 0.35 }`; `PopoverOutline` converts the rem with the root's computed font size, as `Editor.svelte:730` does, so its corner agrees with `nested-radius()` children; `tokens.css` and the module each name the other, and a unit test reads `tokens.css` and checks they agree.
5. **Stages are cut by item, not by folder.** A file can be touched in more than one stage. A change that moves pixels lands with the item that decides it, and stage 2 holds only changes that move none. `global.css` and `styles/` change in whichever stage's item needs them.
6. **One `tool` mixin, on the new hover.** `tray.scss`'s `tool` is split into its look (padding, radius, weight, transition, hover) and its tray-row part (`height: 100%`, `color: var(--primary-text)`). Its hover becomes `--hover-bg` (D4), so the execution dock's Stop, Undo and Test hover with the new tint like every other row and tool; the rest of the dock, tuned in the [Workbench record](./workbench.md) on 2026-10-03, does not change. The `ghost` Button includes the look part, so the two cannot drift.
7. **Buttons keep `bg`, `color` and `variant`.** `cssVar` goes (D5). `bg` and `color` stay as the deliberate exception D5 allows, for a colour that is data: the language's accent on `ProjectCard` and `ContentLinkCard`, each theme's background in `ThemeEditor`. `variant` stays as the one switch D5 keeps for the look, holding only `workbench` once the `classic` layer is folded into it. The `secondary` role's fill becomes the text tint D5 decided, in stage 3; radius, padding, weight and hover stay as they are until the look decision.
8. **The gates.** A capture script, `.cache/design-system/capture.mjs` (git-ignored, as the audit's shots are), drives headless Chrome against a production build served by `pnpm preview`, the same command before and after:
    - Pages at 1440×900 and 390×844, full page: `/`, `/projects`, `/projects/create`, `/documentation`, a language page, an instruction page, a chapter page, a `/documentation/<language>/all` page, `/learn/courses`, a course, a lecture, `/blog`, a blog post, `/changelog`, `/donate`, `/examples`, `/exam`, an exam session, `/embed`, `/chat`.
    - The Workbench at 1440×900 in Cards and in Lines, and at 390×844 and a tablet width: editing, a Debug session after Build, the Explorer's context menu open, Settings open, a Tooltip shown, the search palette open with results, a toast. Its Project is created in the page (`makeProject` and `ProjectStore.addProject`, as the verification recipes describe).
    - Pinned: the clock (`page.clock.setFixedTime`, which also freezes "Edited …", the Log's times and Build durations at a fixed value through a stubbed `performance.now`, or those lines are masked); animations (`screenshot({ animations: 'disabled', caret: 'hide' })`, which settles CSS and Web Animations, Svelte's included); fonts (Google Fonts responses recorded on the first run and replayed, and the shot taken after `document.fonts.ready` once Monaco has mounted); the palette (shot after the embedding model reports ready, or with the model disabled); the home page's demo iframe (its request blocked); `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
    - Each shot is taken twice in a row and must match itself before it counts. `compare -metric AE` against the baseline must report 0 for every pair in stage 2. The baseline is taken at the end of stage 1.

    _As run for stage 2 (2026-11-07):_ the script captures a private dev server (`packages/asm-editor/node_modules/.cache/design-system.config.mts`, port 4197) rather than a build, since a build writes the `build/` and `.svelte-kit/output` folders other sessions share and costs minutes each time; both sides of a comparison use the same server mode. Usage is in the script's header: start the server from `packages/asm-editor` with `node_modules/.bin/vp dev --config node_modules/.cache/design-system.config.mts`, then `node .cache/design-system/capture.mjs shoot <label> [name-regex]` and `node .cache/design-system/capture.mjs compare <a> <b>`. `shots/stage2-after` is stage 3's baseline. Run it on an idle machine: under load a page can hold a half-loaded state for two shots in a row. Phone and tablet Tooltips are shown by hover rather than a long press, and pages that scroll inside themselves (`/chat`, `/examples`, `/embed`, an `/all` page, the exam session) are compared only as far as the screen shows.

## Stage 1: fixes that need no decision

Visible, small and intended. Each a commit:

1. **Undefined variables.** `Card.svelte`'s `--shadow-rgb` becomes `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.2)` (the Card is rebuilt in stage 4); `--border-color` in `InteractiveInstructionEditor.svelte:1114` becomes `var(--wb-line)` (it is inside `.playground`); `--border-color-dark` in `routes/exam/session/+page.svelte:1142` becomes `color-mix(in srgb, var(--tertiary) 60%, transparent)`, since nothing above it declares `--wb-line`. `warning` is added (planning decision 3) as `#d49a30`, which changes nothing in the editor and the Log and turns the Pixel editor's mark from the accent to yellow. Each fixed spot is screenshotted.
2. **Focus ring.** After the reset in `global.css`, `:focus-visible` draws `2px solid var(--accent)` at a 2px offset, except on text-entry elements (text `input`s, `textarea`, `[contenteditable]`), where it is inset (−2px) so it does not overlap neighbours in dense forms; a `focus-ring($inset: false)` mixin gives components the same. Opt-outs where focus is already drawn another way: Monaco (`.monaco-editor :focus-visible`), `HexEditor`'s `.hex-scroller`, `InputCells`' cells. The 75 hand-copied rules stay until their component is touched in stage 3, where identical ones are deleted. Checked by tabbing through IconRail, FileTabs, BottomPanel, SidePanel, TopBar, Explorer, DefaultNavbar, Sidebar, both search fields' clear buttons, `Input`, `Textarea` and the ~15 files of raw inputs, and by clicking into a text field.
3. **Form controls.** `input, textarea, select { font: inherit; color: inherit }` in `global.css`. This resizes every input, select and textarea that set no size of their own, across the app; each is screenshotted, the create page's Name and Description placeholders among them.
4. **Font tokens and `monospace`.** `styles/tokens.css` is created with `--font-ui: Rubik` (alone, as the app writes it today; the generic family is added in stage 3), `--font-read: 'Noto Serif', Rubik, sans-serif` and `--font-mono: 'Fira Code', monospace`. The 24 bare `monospace` declarations in 14 files and the `sans-serif` at `MemoryRenderer.svelte:846` use them. The 11 without a size of their own leave the browser's 13px monospace default for the inherited size and grow; each is screenshotted, and a size from the type scale is set where the growth is wrong.
5. **Fira Code's variable font** (D15, planning decision 2). The five static Fira Code files and `Orienta-Regular.ttf` are deleted from `static/fonts/`.
6. **The exam page's offset.** The cause is found before anything changes: `/exam` pads 3.5rem with no `hasNavbar`, and the navbar is about 3.25rem, so the padding alone should clear it; the candidates are a vertically centred column taller than the viewport and a navbar taller on that page. The fix is checked at both widths.
7. **Scrollbars.** WebKit's track becomes transparent and the thumb's hover a mix of `--scroll-accent` and the text colour. Firefox gets `scrollbar-color: var(--scroll-accent) transparent` and `scrollbar-width: thin` on `html`, inside `@supports not selector(::-webkit-scrollbar)`, since Chrome 121 and later ignore the `::-webkit-scrollbar` rules as soon as `scrollbar-color` is set; `ThemeProvider` writes `--scroll-accent` on `document.documentElement` as well as `<body>`, because Firefox reads the page's scrollbar colour from the root element.
8. **Reduced motion, CSS part.** One `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rule in `global.css` shortens CSS transitions and animations to almost nothing, except the spinners (`BottomPanel:228`, `CompilationNotice:92`, `ExecutionDock:719`, `SearchField:196`, by selector), which keep turning, and the toast's countdown bar, which keeps its length. Svelte's transitions are not reached by it; they are stage 3's item 11.
9. **Dead code.** `button/style.scss` is deleted. The `classic` layer stays until stage 3 folds it into `workbench`.

End of stage: `pnpm check`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test`; screenshots of each fix for the owner; then the stage-2 baseline is captured.

## Stage 2: tokens, with nothing on screen changing

1. **`styles/tokens.css` complete.** The type scale of D11, its phone step-down in one media query at 600px, the breakpoint `contentPages.css` uses; the radii of D12; the shadows of D13 (`--shadow-2` as `tray.scss`'s today, `--shadow-3` as the menus' `0 0.5rem 1.5rem /0.35`, `--shadow-1` as the most common sticky-header shadow); `--dur-fast` 120ms, `--dur-base` 160ms, `--ease-out` (`cubic-bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1)`, `cubicOut`); `--scrim` (black 35%) _(audit)_; `--hover-bg` (`color-mix(in srgb, currentColor 10%, transparent)`) and `--text-muted` (`currentColor` at 65%). Weights stay numbers (400, 500, 600, 700) _(audit)_.
2. **Theme-derived tokens and renames.** `themeCssVariables()` appends `--line` (`--tertiary` 60%), `--line-strong` (`--tertiary`) and `--selected-bg` (accent 20%), and the renames of planning decision 3 land with all their uses. `AgentDocs.svelte`'s local `--line` (a line height) is renamed first, so it cannot shadow the token.
3. **Old names become aliases.** `--wb-line: var(--line)` and `--tray-line: var(--line)`; `--hex-line`, `--dock-line`, `--preview-line` and `--pixel-line` give way to `var(--line)` at their uses; `--wb-radius: var(--r-control)`; `contentPages.css`'s `--content-*-size` become aliases of the `--fs-*` steps, `.learn-pages` still redefining its own. The Lines style's overrides keep winning, as `workbench.css` declares through `:where()`; the Lines captures confirm it. The home page's local `--line`, `--line-strong`, `--muted`, `--serif` and `--mono` stay this stage.
4. **Raw values equal to a token are replaced**, file by file. The interface-end font sizes (0.65, 0.75, 0.85, 1rem) everywhere; the page-end sizes (1.1, 1.2, 1.4, 1.8, 2.75rem) only inside `.content-pages`, where the same values already step down at 600px, since elsewhere a token would step down on phones where the raw value does not (`/projects`' 1.8rem title would become 1.6rem). Font stacks exactly equal to a token's (`Rubik` alone to `--font-ui`; `Rubik, sans-serif` waits for stage 3, as it differs on → and ✓). ## Risks

- `--hover-bg` and `--text-muted` come from `currentColor`, so a row whose own text is muted or in the accent gets a weaker or tinted hover. Where that shows, the colour goes on an inner element rather than on the row.
- The global focus ring can double up with a component's own ring until stage 3 removes it, and can appear on elements that draw focus their own way beyond the three opted out; each is opted out as found.
- `font: inherit` and the end of the 13px monospace default resize controls and code text app-wide in stage 1; the screenshots of that stage are the check.
- The stage-2 gate can flicker in ways not yet pinned; a difference is investigated, never accepted, and the self-match of each shot catches flicker before it reaches the comparison.
- `tray.scss`'s mixins feed the dock, the prompts, the toasts and the palette at once: a change to them is checked on all four.
- `motion()` sets a Svelte transition's duration to 0, which still fires `introend` and `outroend`; anything waiting for one is checked once.
