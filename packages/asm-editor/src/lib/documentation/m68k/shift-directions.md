## What shift directions are

The shift logical, shift arithmetical and rotate, instructions use the directions to specify in which direction the bits are shifted.

{directions}

Those instructions are: [lsl](/documentation/m68k/instruction/lsl) [lsr](/documentation/m68k/instruction/lsr) [rol](/documentation/m68k/instruction/rol) [ror](/documentation/m68k/instruction/ror) [asl](/documentation/m68k/instruction/asl) [asr](/documentation/m68k/instruction/asr)
